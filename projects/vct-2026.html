What this dashboard does

  • Filters every panel by tournament — all ten VCT 2026 events, or the full season combined — recalculating every chart on selection.
  • Ranks players by Rating in a scoreboard-style table with tiered color chips, alongside ACS, K/D, HS%, and ADR.
  • Shows agent pick rate as horizontal bars and map win rate as attack-vs-defense split bars.
  • Builds team standings with match record and map differential, sorted by performance.
  • Summarizes multi-kill distribution (2K–5K/Aces) and spike plant/defuse totals per tournament.
  • Gracefully handles tournaments with incomplete source data by showing a clear empty state instead of breaking.
